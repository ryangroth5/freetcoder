Write a CodeSignal General Coding Assessment-style question.

- Implementation- and simulation-heavy rather than a clever trick. The candidate
  should mostly be translating carefully-specified rules into correct code.
- Longer prose than LeetCode: spell the rules out precisely, including how ties,
  empty inputs and boundaries behave. Ambiguity is the failure mode here.
- Always include one fully worked example walking through the rules step by step.
- Concrete but neutral framing (a grid, a log, a queue of events).
- Do NOT include a hint: this format gives candidates no support.
